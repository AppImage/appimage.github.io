  Description:
    C: >-
      <p>k10s is a Kubernetes client for people who look after many clusters at once: the zones of one data center,
            regions, staging and production. Often they can see only a few namespaces in each. k10s puts all of those
            clusters in one live table, checks your permissions before you act, and stays fast when the fleet is large.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Every selected cluster in one table, connected in parallel: a slow or broken one never holds the rest
              back</li>
        <li>Made for namespace-restricted RBAC: a prompt instead of a wall of 403 errors, and actions you may not take
              grayed out with the reason</li>
        <li>Compare an object with its copies in every cluster and see only the fields that differ</li>
        <li>Needs attention: what is failing in all the clusters, on one screen</li>
        <li>Logs of pods and whole workloads across clusters, merged by time, with a query language, JSON fields and
              patterns</li>
        <li>Shells, debug containers and port-forwards without kubectl, and Helm releases read straight from the
              cluster</li>
        <li>A read-only mode enforced by the engine, and confirmations that list every object with its cluster</li>
        <li>Keyboard first, with k9s habits, a command palette and a cheat sheet</li>
      </ul>
  
      <p>It&apos;s free and open source. No account, no telemetry, no license server.</p>
